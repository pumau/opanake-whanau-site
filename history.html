The story of our whenua and our whānau

From Irihapeti (Peti Betty) Howearth née Patuawa at Mamaranui, Kaihu, to the nine branches of today — this is the thread that connects us all back to the whenua.

"He aha te mea nui o te ao? He tangata, he tangata, he tangata."

What is the most important thing in the world? It is people, it is people, it is people.
