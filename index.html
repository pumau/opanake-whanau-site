Whānau · Whenua · Whakapapa

A home for the descendants of Irihapeti Patuawa

Ōpanake 1C South 6B is our shared whenua at Mamaranui, Kaihu. This site is bringing the nine sibling branches together in one place, for hui, whakapapa, the archive, and each other.

Our tupuna

Irihapeti Patuawa

Also known as Peti, or Peti Betty. Howearth née Patuawa, and connected to Mamaranui, Kaihu.

Born in 1894, Irihapeti is the tupuna all nine branches of our whānau descend from. Her name appears in Māori Land Court records for Ōpanake 1C South 6B, where it is sometimes spelt Riripeti.

The records place her on this whenua for much of the twentieth century. A 1916 Court order names her as owner of block 6B. In 1943 the Court awarded her a replacement 6B block, recorded at about 73 acres, and a 1958 owner list shows her holding it in full. In 1987 the Court dealt with her estate and the land passed on to her successors.

That is the paper trail, and it is all this page claims. It cannot tell us what she was like, what she cared about, or how she is remembered in your branch. Every one of us carries a different story of Nanny Irihapeti, and none is more right than another. If you have a photo, a memory or some whakapapa to add, share it in the Forum or on the Photos page.

Source: Māori Land Court records for Ōpanake 1C South 6B. We are still confirming some details with the Court, so this may be corrected.
