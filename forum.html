Our kōrero guidelines

This forum is for the whole whānau, across all nine branches. Let's keep it a place where everyone feels welcome to join in.

Ideas are welcome. Share what you're thinking, ask questions, and talk things through. We won't all see things the same way, and that's fine.

Connection is welcome. Stories, memories, photos, updates and a simple kia ora all help us stay close.

Swearing and hate speech are not welcome. Posts containing them are filtered out automatically. If that happens you will be asked to reword what you wrote, then you can post it again.

Please keep other people's personal details private unless they've said it's okay to share them. An admin may remove anything that doesn't fit these guidelines.
